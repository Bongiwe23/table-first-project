Recipe for Amagwinya

  • Yields: Approx. 12 to 15 fat cakes
  • Prep Time: 10 minutes + 1.5 hours rising
  • Cook Time: 15 minutes

Please find the list of ingredients below .


LIST OF INGREDIENTS FOR AMAGWINYA.
ITEMS WEIGHT
Cake flour 4 cups
Sugar 4 tbsp
Salt 1 tsp
Instant Yeast 1 packet(10g)
Warm Water 1.5 to 2 cups
Cooking Oil(for dough) 1 tbsp
Sunflower Oil(for frying) 750ml to 1L

INSTRUCTIONS:

  1. Mix Dry Ingredients: In a large bowl, sift the flour and combine with sugar, salt, and yeast.
  2. Form Dough: Make a well in the center. Gradually add the warm water and 1 tbsp of oil. Mix with your hands until a soft, slightly sticky dough forms.
  3. Knead: Transfer to a lightly floured surface. Knead for about 5–10 minutes until the dough is smooth and elastic
  4. First Rise: Place the dough in an oiled bowl, cover with a clean cloth or cling wrap, and leave in a warm spot for 1 hour or until doubled in size.
  5. Shape: Punch the dough down to release air. Pinch off tennis-ball-sized pieces and roll them into smooth rounds. Let these rest for another 15–30 minutes for extra fluffiness.
  6. Fry: Heat oil in a deep pot over medium heat. Carefully drop a few balls in (do not overcrowd). Fry for about 3 minutes per side until golden brown.
  7. Drain: Remove with a slotted spoon and place on paper towels to drain excess oil

Pro Tip: If the oil is too hot, they will be raw inside;if it's too cold,they will be greasy.

Serving Suggestions

  • Savory: Fill with mince curry, polony and atchaar, or cheese.
  • Sweet: Enjoy warm with butter and jam or a drizzle of syrup
